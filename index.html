Installation & Setup

Follow these steps to connect your game to your stream.

1

Launch Crowd Control Panel

Download and launch the Control Panel on your PC. Connect your Twitch account to enable viewer integration.

2

Place Mod Files on SD Card or Emulator

Download the SMO Crowd Control mod. Copy the atmosphere/ folder to your Nintendo Switch SD card root, or import the contents into your emulator's mod directory.

3

Establish Connection

Launch Super Mario Odyssey (v1.0.0). In the Crowd Control PC client, input your console IP address, and start your session.

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical requirements and common issues.

Do I need a hacked Nintendo Switch?

Yes, if playing on hardware, your console must run Atmosphère custom firmware to load memory patches and communicate with the network client. Alternatively, you can use a PC emulator like Ryujinx.

Is this mod affiliated with Nintendo?

No. This is an open-source fan project developed by independent modders for community use. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with Nintendo.
